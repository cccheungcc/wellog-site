Log your day.

Scan a meal, log a glass of water, check your blood pressure — then see how the week actually went. A simple daily health log for iPhone.

Coming soon to the App Store

iPhone · iOS 17 or later · Apple Health supported

📷

Scan your meals

Point the camera at a plate and get calories, protein, carbs, fat and sodium in seconds. Adjust the portion in grams or millilitres, or set how much of a shared dish you actually ate.

💧

One tap for the rest

Water straight from the Home Screen widget. Blood pressure, body weight and alcohol in seconds — with calorie and unit estimates worked out for you.

❤️

Apple Health, both ways

Reads and writes blood pressure, heart rate, weight, steps, energy, nutrients and water. Only with your permission, and only what you allow.

📈

See the trends

Week and month charts for blood pressure, nutrition, water and weight, plus a calendar showing which days landed over or under target.

🎯

Targets that fit you

Calorie, protein, sodium, water and alcohol targets from your own profile and goal — adjusted if you note blood pressure medication.

🔒

Your data stays yours

Logs live on your device and in Apple Health. Meal photos are sent for analysis only when you choose to scan. Export everything to CSV whenever you like.

Free to log. Subscribe to scan without limits.

Manual food entry, water, weight, blood pressure and alcohol are always free. Photo scanning is free for your first 30 meals; a monthly subscription unlocks unlimited scanning.
